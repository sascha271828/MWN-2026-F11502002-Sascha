## 1 Literature Review
The original Mangold et al. remains as the basis for the literature review and is therefore also included.
Further two more papers for the Literature review were selected based upon the following criteria:
1. Analyse 802.11e EDCA with per AC metrics (throughput, delay)
2. Contain a simulation section with an explicit parameter table, so that the setting can be translated into ns-3
3. extend what Mangold et al. only describe qualitatively (AIFS, CW per AC, virtual collision, retry limit)

Both additional papers refrence the Mangold group and the Bianchi's DCF model (will be refrenced in the actual literature review in more detail).

- [Analysis of IEEE 802.11e for QoS support in wireless LANs](doc/literature-review.md/#1-analysis-of-ieee-80211e-for-qos-support-in-wireless-lans)

- [Performance analysis of IEEE 802.11e contention-based channel access](doc/literature-review.md/#2-performance-analysis-of-ieee-80211e-contention-based-channel-access)

- [Throughput and delay performance of IEEE 802.11e enhanced distributed channel access (EDCA) under saturation condition](doc/literature-review.md/#3-throughput-and-delay-performance-of-ieee-80211e-enhanced-distributed-channel-access-edca-under-saturation-condition)
